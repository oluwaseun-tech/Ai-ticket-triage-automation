# Privacy and safe sharing

CareFlow Health is a fictional portfolio scenario. Use synthetic test tickets in all public screenshots, sample data and demonstrations.

Before publishing workflow exports or screenshots:

- Remove credential IDs, names, tokens and secret values.
- Replace spreadsheet IDs and private resource identifiers with placeholders.
- Hide customer names, email addresses and support descriptions.
- Check URLs, browser tabs, execution logs and node parameters for private information.
- Confirm you have permission to publish every screenshot and workflow file.
- Keep customer communication under human review.
- Avoid implying that the workflow is deployed for a real healthcare provider.

The public workflow export in this repository has had its spreadsheet identifier, cached spreadsheet URLs and credential references removed. Reconnect accounts after importing it into n8n.
